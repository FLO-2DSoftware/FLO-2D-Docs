=============================================
Storm Drain
=============================================

FLO-2D simulates an exchange process between the surface and the storm drain system. Flow can travel into the storm drain
and back to the surface as drainage or backwater under pressure. The following case studies demonstrate the use of the
storm drain feature in FLO-2D to represent the interaction between surface flow and subsurface drainage infrastructure.
The examples include both drainage and backwater scenarios, illustrating how the model can capture the complex hydraulic
behavior associated with storm drain systems during flood events.
